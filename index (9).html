Every observation has equal weight. Its influence depends on its distance from the pivot.

Why distance matters

Each observation creates a moment: weight × signed distance from the pivot. All weights are 1 here.

An observation 4 units away has four times the moment of an observation 1 unit away—not sixteen times. Squared distances belong to a different property of the mean.

Keyboard accessible: select a weight and use ← / →. Values are constrained to 0–10. Works offline; no dependencies.
